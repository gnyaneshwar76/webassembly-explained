Privacy

This site has no accounts, forms, cookies or analytics. It does not collect or store any personal data.

The demo runs entirely in your browser. The numbers you type are processed on your device and are never sent anywhere. Fonts are served from this site, not from a third party.

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub, as the host, may log technical data such as your IP address when you load a page. See GitHub's privacy statement.

This is a plain description of how the site works, not legal advice. Last updated 2 November 2026.
